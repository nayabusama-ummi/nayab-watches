NAYAB | Haute Horlogerie & Contemporary Pakistani Watchmaking

NAYAB is an independent luxury watch house crafting mechanical timepieces defined by patience, regional metallurgy, and generational permanence in Lahore, Pakistan. Our atelier explores mechanical time through the subcontinent's traditions of precision craft, material artistry, and architectural balance.

A Legacy Measured in Generations

Built with an old-world respect for manual horology, NAYAB pairs in-house calibres with 18k rose gold, grade 5 titanium, honey gold, and damascened steel cases. Every mechanical movement is hand-finished, adjusted for chronometer precision, and individually registered in the Lahore ledger of provenance.
